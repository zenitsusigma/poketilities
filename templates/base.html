How to Play

Who's That Pokemon?

Guess the Pokemon before its silhouette fully reveals. Pick a difficulty (Easy hints the word length, Hard hides everything and adds a timer), choose generations, and toggle Mega Evolutions or Regional Forms for a harder pool. Type anywhere on the page to answer, Ctrl+Delete clears your guess, and you've got 3 lives per run.

Pokedle

Wordle-style guessing: type a Pokemon name, and each guess shows how close you are on type, generation, height, and weight, with arrows pointing toward the answer. Unlimited guesses, or hit Quit to reveal the answer and start fresh.

Stat Battle

Two random Pokemon, one random stat (HP, Attack, Speed, etc). Guess which one's higher to keep your streak going.

Akinator

Think of a Pokemon and answer Yes, No, or Unsure to each question. It'll try to guess what you're thinking of in around 9 questions or fewer — and gets a second guess if the first one's wrong.

Pokedex

Click any numbered slot to reveal that Pokemon's sprite and guess its name. Correct answers stay filled in for good, so you can work toward completing an entire generation over time.

Quiz

15 multiple-choice Pokemon trivia questions, shuffled into a new order each run.

Calculator

Pick one or two types and instantly see everything that's 4x or 2x super effective, resisted, or a complete dead end against that combo.

Multiplayer

Create a room and share the code, or join one a friend made. Everyone in the room sees the same Pokemon at once — first correct guess wins the round.
